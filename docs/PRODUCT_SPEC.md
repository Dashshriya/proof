# PROOF — Product Spec

## Core promise

The learning objective stays fixed. The route to demonstrating understanding can change.

## Core loop

1. Define objective.
2. Decompose objective into concepts.
3. Ask an initial question.
4. Analyze the response against concepts.
5. Identify missing evidence.
6. Select a lower-expression-load or alternative probe.
7. Reassess.
8. Produce an evidence map.

## Guardrails

- Do not diagnose.
- Do not infer a medical condition.
- Do not claim a student understands a concept without evidence.
- Do not lower the learning objective merely because a student struggles.
- Keep uncertainty visible.
- Prefer one small next question over a long explanation.
